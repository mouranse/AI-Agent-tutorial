The 2022 FIFA World Cup was held in Qatar from November 20 to December 18, 2022. It was the first World Cup hosted in the Arab world and the second in Asia. This was the last World Cup to feature 32 teams. Argentina won their third World Cup title, defeating France in the final match. This victory marked Lionel Messi's first World Cup title.
